# Cover Letter & Interview Templates (for Applicants)

> Reference file. Load only when the user needs a cover letter, application message, interview follow-up, or offer negotiation script. Context pointer: *Resume & Cover Letter branch / Interview Prep branch / Job Search Strategy & Offer Negotiation branch: see reference for full templates.*

## Template Types

### 1. Cold Application Cover Letter (Email or Portal)

Use when applying via a company's careers page, email, or direct submission. Adoption-first: the resume is attached; the cover letter sells team adoption wins, with the GitHub link as proof of AI literacy.

```
## Application: [Role] at [Company]

**To:** [Hiring Manager Name] or [Team]

### Hook (1 sentence)
Lead with the adoption win that's relevant to them.

I'm applying for the [Role] role at [Company]. I've shipped 40+ AI systems
and gotten the teams behind them to change how they work: [1-sentence
adoption win with metric relevant to their challenge].

### Evidence (the core)
I'm change management certified (CCMP). I've delivered AI workflows that
automated manual processes, then trained the teams to run them. Proof I
understand the tech: github.com/armchairfuturist-code/[repo]. The adoption
numbers prove I can get people to use it.

### Bridge
Why [Company]? [1-2 sentences specific to their product, blog post, or
AI adoption challenge: demonstrate you've done your homework.]

### Close
I'd love 15 minutes to walk you through how I've helped teams adopt AI
workflows and talk about how I'd approach [their specific adoption challenge].

Best,
Alex Myers
github.com/armchairfuturist-code
```

**Key rule:** Never send a generic cover letter. The adoption win must be **relevant to what the company does**, and the repo must back it. If you don't have a directly relevant repo, use the closest analogous project and say "same architecture, different domain."

### 2. Warm Intro Message (DM or Email via Referral)

Use when someone at the company (or a mutual connection) agrees to make an intro. Shorter, warmer, still adoption-first.

```
**Subject:** [Referral Name] suggested I reach out

Hi [Name],

[Referral Name] suggested I connect. I help teams adopt AI: 40+ systems
shipped, teams trained across 4 continents, change management certified.
I'm exploring contract and retainer engagements (paid to my LLC) with
companies working on [industry/sector], and [Company]'s work on [product]
caught my eye.

Would you have 10 minutes this week to chat? I'd love to hear how you're
approaching AI adoption at [Company] and share what's worked for my clients.

Best,
Alex Myers
github.com/armchairfuturist-code
```

---

### 3. LinkedIn InMail / DM (Cold to Recruiter or Hiring Manager)

Use when direct-messaging a recruiter, hiring manager, or tech lead on LinkedIn. Short, high-signal, one-click to GitHub.

```
Hi [Name],

I get teams to adopt AI: 40+ systems shipped, teams trained across 4
continents, change management certified. [Company]'s [product/team] looks
like a place where adoption is the hard part. That's the work I do.

Would you have a few minutes to see if there's a match?

Alex
github.com/armchairfuturist-code
```

**Note:** This is a bolt: short enough to read on mobile. The adoption claim leads; the GitHub link backs it.

---

### 4. Follow-Up After Application (No Response)

Send 7-10 days after applying if no response. Keep it short. Don't re-pitch. Remind them of the adoption win and where the proof lives.

```
**Subject:** Follow-up: [Role] applicant: 40+ AI systems shipped, teams adopted

Hi [Name],

Following up on my application for the [Role] role. The short version: I ship
AI systems and get teams to use them. Proof: github.com/armchairfuturist-code/[repo].

If the role is still open, I'd love 15 minutes to walk you through how my
client teams adopted these workflows and how I'd approach adoption at [Company].

Best,
Alex Myers
github.com/armchairfuturist-code
```

### 5. Thank-You After Interview

Send within 4 hours of the interview. Reference the conversation.

```

discussed: e.g., their architecture challenge, the product roadmap, the team
structure].

github.com/armchairfuturist-code/[repo], and the adoption results behind it
are the part I'd happily walk you through.

I'm excited about the possibility of contributing to [Company]. Happy to
answer any follow-up questions or do a deeper walkthrough.

Best,
Alex Myers
github.com/armchairfuturist-code
```

### 6. Offer Evaluation & Negotiation Script

Use when you have an offer in hand and need to evaluate or negotiate.

```
## Offer Evaluation: [Company]

### The Offer
- Role: [Title]
- Rate/base: [$X]
- Equity: [X% / X options at $Y strike]
- Other: [Signing bonus, benefits, etc.]

### Evaluation
| Factor | Good | Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Rate/base | [market for stage?] | [below market?] |
| Equity | [stage-appropriate %?] | [below expected?] |
| Role scope | [aligned with growth?] | [narrower than discussed?] |
| Team/Manager | [strong signal?] | [unknown?] |
| Product fit | [can use your skills day 1?] | [pivot risk?] |
| Flexibility | [remote, hours, start date] | [rigid?] |

### Negotiation Script (if needed)

**Option 1: Base salary**
> "I'm excited about the role and the team. Based on the scope and my
> experience shipping [relevant systems], I was hoping for something closer
> to [$X + Y%]. Is there flexibility on base?"

**Option 2: Base is fixed, negotiate equity/title/flexibility**
> "I understand the base is firm. Could we look at equity or a 6-month
> performance review with a defined increase?"

**Option 3: Multiple offers (only if true)**
> "I have another offer on the table, but [Company] is my first choice. If
> you can match [$X], I'll accept today."

**Option 4: LLC / contractor framing**
> "I contract through Alex Myers Consulting LLC: no payroll taxes, no
> benefits overhead on your side. For a [6/12]-month engagement I'd propose
> [$X/hr or $Y/month retainer], Net 30, with a rate review at 6 months."
```

### 7. Take-Home Project README Template

If the company sends a take-home assignment, use this as the README structure:

```
# [Project Name]: Take-Home for [Company]

## What it does
[1-2 sentences]

## Architecture
[Key decisions and trade-offs. What did you choose and why?]

## How to run
[Clear instructions]

## What I'd add with more time
[Shows you think beyond the spec: optional, strong signal]
```

## Quick Reference: Which Template for Which Situation

| Situation | Use Template |
|---|---|
| Applying via careers page / email | #1: Cold Application Cover Letter |
| Warm intro from a connection | #2: Warm Intro Message |
| Cold DM on LinkedIn | #3: LinkedIn InMail |
| No response after 7-10 days | #4: Follow-Up After Application |
| Just finished an interview | #5: Thank-You After Interview |
| Got an offer, need to evaluate/negotiate | #6: Offer Evaluation & Negotiation |
| Company sent a take-home | #7: Take-Home README |

**Key rule for all templates:** Every message leads adoption-first and includes a GitHub repo link as supporting proof. If the template placeholder says `[project]` and you don't have a directly relevant repo, use the closest analogous project and note "same architecture, different domain."
